# USM Harvard Formatting Rules (Harvard-USM Ver 2020)

Templates for each source type. Use the template, fill in the fields, and mark any missing field in red as described in SKILL.md, Step 4.

Examples are from the lecture slides "Referencing" (Assoc. Prof. Dr WM Zahiruddin) unless marked (fictitious).

---

## Journals

### Article with DOI
```
Surname, A. B., Surname, C. D. & Surname, E. (Year). Article title in sentence case. Journal Name, Volume(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xx
```
Example:
```
Ismail, T. A., Sulaiman, Z., Jalil, R., Muda, W. M. & Man, N. N. (2012). Breast milk expression among formally employed women in urban and rural Malaysia: A qualitative study. Int Breastfeed J, 7(1), 11. https://doi.org/10.1186/1746-4358-7-11
```

### Article without DOI
```
Surname, A. B., Surname, C. D. & Surname, E. (Year). Article title in sentence case. Journal Name, Volume(Issue), pages.
```
Example:
```
Anita, S., Zahiruddin, W. M., Sa'iah, A., Rahimah, M. A. & Sha'ari, B. N. (2007). HIV/AIDS knowledge, attitudes and risk behaviours among Orang Asli in Peninsular Malaysia. Med J Malaysia, 62(3), 227-233.
```

### Article with an article number (no page range)
Write the article number in place of the pages. Do not mark it as missing.
```
Jamali-Dolatabad, M., Sarbakhsh, P. & Sadeghi-Bazargani, H. (2021). Hidden patterns among the fatally injured pedestrians in an Iranian population: Application of categorical principal component analysis (CATPCA). BMC Public Health, 21(1), 1149. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-021-11212-x
```

### Article in a supplement issue
Write `Volume(Suppl. N)`:
```
Inj Prev, 26(Suppl. 1), i46-i56. https://doi.org/10.1136/injuryprev-2019-043302
```

### Journal with no issue numbers
If the journal publishes by volume only, write the volume and pages with no issue:
```
Mohan, D. (2019). Traffic safety: Rights and obligations. Accid Anal Prev, 128, 159-163. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aap.2019.04.010
```

### Article with missing pages or issue
If no page range and no article number are given, mark the missing field in red:
```
Abdullah, N. D. S., Mahadi, M. R. & Baharudin, M. R. (2023). Development of mobile application in assessing commuting accident risk (CommuRisk) amongst commuters at Klang Valley. Malaysian Journal of Medicine & Health Sciences, 19(3), <span style="color:red">**[MISSING: pages]**</span>. https://doi.org/10.47836/mjmhs.19.3.14
```
Do not take the article number from the DOI (such as `14` in `mjmhs.19.3.14`). Use an article number only when the source states it. Otherwise ask the author for the page range or article number.

## Books

### Book
```
Surname, A. B. & Surname, C. (Year). Book title (2nd ed.). Place: Publisher.
```
Leave out the edition if it is the first edition.

Example:
```
Fletcher, R., Fletcher, S. & Wagner, E. (1998). Clinical epidemiology, the essentials (2nd ed.). Baltimore, USA: Williams and Wilkins.
```

### Book chapter (edited book)
```
Surname, A. B. (Year). Chapter title. In: Editor Surname, A. B. (Ed.), Book title (pp. xx-xx). Place: Publisher.
```
Use `(Eds.)` for two or more editors. In the example below the editors are listed as one group, as shown in the slides.

Example:
```
Norsa'adah, B. & Nik Rosmawati, N. H. (2011). Non-parametric tests. In: Nik Rosmawati, N. H., Mohd Ismail, I. & Rohana, A. J. (Eds.), Statistics Made Easy. Basic Statistical Steps in Medical & Health Research. Kota Bharu: Department of Community Medicine, pp. 95-116.
```

## Conference

### Conference paper or proceedings
```
Surname, A. B. (Year). Paper title. Proceedings from Conference name, pp. xx-xx. Place: Publisher.
```
- This follows the Harvard-USM EndNote output (`Proceedings from ...`).
- If the conference name already starts with "Proceedings of", do not repeat the word. Write `Proceedings from the Fifth International Conference...`, not `Proceedings from Proceedings of the Fifth International Conference...`.
- If pages or publisher are missing, mark them in red.

Example (from a USM MPH report, corrected):
```
Chakraborty, A., Mitra, S. & Maitra, B. (2022). A comprehensive review of motorcyclists' riding behaviour and safety. Proceedings from the Fifth International Conference of Transportation Research Group of India, <span style="color:red">**[MISSING: pages]**</span>. Singapore: <span style="color:red">**[MISSING: publisher]**</span>.
```

## Theses and Dissertations

### Thesis or dissertation (print)
```
Surname, A. B. (Year). Title of thesis (Dissertation). Institution name, Place.
```

Example (fictitious):
```
Ahmad, S. (2019). Factors influencing vaccination uptake among caregivers in Kelantan (Dissertation). Universiti Sains Malaysia, Kubang Kerian.
```

### Unpublished student dissertation
Use `(Unpublished work)` instead of `(Dissertation)`:
```
Surname, A. B. (Year). Title of dissertation (Unpublished work). Institution name, Place.
```

### Thesis found online
```
Surname, A. B. (Year). Title of thesis. Retrieved from: URL [Accessed dd Month yyyy].
```
- This follows the Harvard-USM EndNote output: a thesis read online is formatted like a webpage.
- Do not add `(Dissertation)`, the degree type, or the institution. Do not mark them as missing.
- Write `Retrieved from: URL` with one space after the colon. EndNote often outputs `Retrieved from:https://...` with no space. Add the space.
- The access date is required. If it is missing, mark it in red as `[MISSING: access date]`.

Example (from user's test case):
```
Adenan, A. F. (2018). The relationship between fatigue factors, job factors and environmental factors towards commuting accidents among nurses in hospital. Retrieved from: https://etd.uum.edu.my/id/eprint/8257 [Accessed 17 February 2024].
```

## Reports and Software

### Report
```
Organisation. (Year). Report title (Report No. xx). Place: Publisher.
```
Example (fictitious):
```
World Health Organization. (2013). Global status report on noncommunicable diseases. Geneva: WHO.
```

### Computer programme
```
Surname, A. B. (Year). Program name (Version x.x) [Computer software]. Publisher.
```

## Web

### Webpage or electronic source
```
Organisation or Author. (Year). Page title. Retrieved from: URL [Accessed dd Month yyyy].
```
Example:
```
Loney, P. L., Chambers, L. W., Bennett, K. J., Roberts, J. G. & Stratford, P. W. (2004). Critical Appraisal of the Health Research Literature Prevalence or Incidence of a Health Problem. Retrieved from: http://www.phac-aspc.gc.ca/ [Accessed 17 March 2009].
```
Note: the slide uses Title Case for this webpage title. The sentence-case rule applies to articles and books, not webpages.

### Website with no author
Use the title in place of the author, and cite it in text with the title in double quotes:
```
("New Child Vaccine," 2001)
```

## Other Types

### Translated work
```
Surname, A. B. (Year). Title (C. D. Translator, Trans.). Place: Publisher.
```

### Reprinted work
Add `(Reprinted from: ...)` after the title.

### Personal communication
In text only. Do not add to the reference list.
```
(J. D. Black, personal communication, May 30, 2013)
```

### Secondary source
In text: `(Miller, 1953, as cited in Agrios, 2005)`. In the reference list, add only Agrios (2005), the source that was read.

---

## Authors Above 20

For 21 or more authors:
```
Surname, A., Surname, B., ... (list authors 1 to 19), ..., Last Author, Z. (Year). Title...
```
